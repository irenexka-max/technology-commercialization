## 🌟 Project Overview | 项目概述

This portfolio highlights my practical experience in **technology commercialization, intellectual property operations, and university–industry innovation** through the 2021 Intellectual Property Operations Practice Program at Hunan University.

During the program, I participated in technology transfer activities and contributed to the commercialization of a **cross-linked hyaluronic acid technology project**.

I obtained a Junior Technology Broker Certificate in China and received the program's Outstanding Participant recognition. Following the program, I was retained in a supporting administrative role to continue assisting university technology transfer activities.

The experience strengthened my understanding of how research outcomes move toward practical applications and how coordination among different stakeholders can help advance commercialization.

**中文简介**

本项目展示我在湖南大学2021年度知识产权运营实践项目中的科技成果转化经验。通过参与学校知识产权运营和科技成果转化工作，我协助推动了“交联透明质酸”技术项目的转化落地，获得初级技术经纪人证书，并获评“2021年度湖南大学知识产权运营实践项目优秀学员”。

项目结束后，我作为优秀学员留任助管，继续参与学校科技成果转化相关工作。

这段经历使我从学术研究之外，进一步接触到技术成果与实际应用之间的连接过程，积累了项目协作、沟通协调与成果转化实践经验。

## 💡 Business Challenge | 核心业务问题

**How can research innovations be translated into practical value?**

如何推动科研创新成果从实验室走向实际应用？

高校拥有丰富的科研成果，但科研成果并不会自动形成具有应用价值的产品或技术方案。科技成果转化往往涉及多个环节，包括技术价值识别、知识产权管理、应用场景探索、合作主体沟通以及项目实施协调。

这些环节需要技术、科研、组织管理与实际应用需求之间的有效衔接。

通过参与科技成果转化实践，我认识到，推动创新落地不仅需要理解技术本身，还需要识别合作机会、协调不同参与方，并将目标转化为可以推进的具体任务。

## 🔄 From Research to Commercialization | 项目实践启示

This experience changed how I approach innovation projects.

I learned that technical potential alone does not guarantee practical impact. Successful implementation also depends on understanding application needs, aligning stakeholders, coordinating resources, and maintaining execution momentum.

The same principles are highly relevant to business development:

**Identify opportunities → Connect stakeholders → Coordinate execution → Create practical value**

这段经历让我认识到，技术创新和商业落地之间需要一套有效的组织与协作机制。商务拓展不仅是发现机会，更需要持续推动机会转化为实际成果。
